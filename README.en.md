# WODHARD

*[Version française](README.md)*

WODHARD is a WOD tracking app for CrossFit and Hyrox, on your phone: your program, the timer, your scores, your progress, and a coach who prepares your sessions.

**Open the app: https://wip06.github.io/WodHard/**

The app's interface is in French; the documentation is available in English.

## What it does

- **Program and timer**: plan your WODs (AMRAP, EMOM, For Time, Hyrox, strength, multi-block sessions) and do them with a timer that guides you round by round.
- **Scores and records**: each session is saved with your RPE and round splits, and the app tracks your records and benchmark WODs.
- **Progress**: your week, your consistency, your training load, your other sports and your goals, down to a race plan for a Hyrox.
- **Coach**: it prepares your sessions and programs from your profile and history, in the app or in your usual AI app.

## Principles

- **No account, no server**: your data stays on your phone, and you back it up yourself.
- **Installable** on your home screen, and usable offline.
- **The coach is optional.** In the app, it uses your own Anthropic API key, paid as you go.
